### **Actives:**

Unskilled Attack (-1 Wanted Level) - Select a node, white or green. Leaves a log.

Fake Escort (-1 Wanted Level) - Escort a target operative, but leave as soon as trouble comes, letting the original target die or get arrested. Visit and occupy them.

Interrogate (3 charges) - Select a target operative and find out what they did last night. Visit and occupy them.

Disorganized Murder (+1 Wanted Level) - Select an operative and murder them. Visit the target.

### **Passives:**

Altar of Heresy - Fake Escort will kill any operative trying to murder the target.

Blood for the Blood God - Disorganized Murder occupies the target.
