### **Actives:**

Hack Target (Moderate, -1 Wanted Level) - Select a white node and hack it, dealing 3 HP. Leaves a log.

Harden Node (Moderate, +1 Wanted Level) - Select a white node, heal the node by 3 HP. Leaves a log.

Frame (2 charges, -1 Wanted Level) - Select a target operative. Change their role to be a specific role, which they will then have all intel abilities follow that frame. Visit them.

Experienced Hiding (3 charges) - Move your hideout, protecting yourself unless you are occupied.

### **Passives:**

Rent Free - Avoid the first arrest attempt and murder attempt on you.

Metaverse Enjoyer - You can choose to frame yourself as any role in the game at the start.

Social Media Handler - You can change your operative name at the start.

Liar, Liar - You use a fake claim and lie about what you do.
