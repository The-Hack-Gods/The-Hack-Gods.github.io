### **Actives:**

Temporary Holding Cell (1 charge) - Select a target operative and jail them for the night. They will receive a similar outcome as Bouncer, like if you visit and occupy them without revealing yourself, and anyone who visits them will be occupied, like a Misdirection. Gain a charge of Dungeon Kill.

Dungeon Kill (0 charges) - Kill the target in the dungeon. If you do not do this within the turn you gain this charge, the operative will be released the next night.

Desperate Measures (1 charge) - Turn into an Improvised Hacker.

### **Passives:**

None.
